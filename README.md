# 🎓 Project 1: Udemy Course & Revenue Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzed Udemy course data to identify opportunities for increasing revenue.

The analysis explored relationships between course subject, pricing, subscriber volume, course level, content duration, and ratings to better understand which course characteristics were associated with stronger performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective was to determine:

> **How and where can Udemy increase revenue?**

The analysis examined several questions, including:

- Which course subjects attract the most subscribers?
- How does course level relate to subscriber volume?
- How do course prices vary across subjects?
- How does content duration vary by subject?
- What patterns exist between course ratings, subjects, and subscriber activity?

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared for analysis using **Google Sheets**.

Data preparation included:

- Removing duplicate records
- Removing blank records
- Standardizing column headers
- Standardizing course-level categories
- Reviewing the dataset for consistency before analysis

Pivot tables and charts were then created to explore relationships within the data before developing visualizations in Tableau.

---

## 📊 Analysis & Visualization

The analysis explored several dimensions of Udemy course performance, including:

- Subscribers by subject
- Subscribers by course level
- Average course price by subject
- Average content duration
- Course ratings
- Course popularity

**Google Sheets** was used for initial exploration and pivot-table analysis, while **Tableau** was used to create visualizations and communicate the findings.

---

## 🔎 Key Findings

Several patterns emerged from the analysis:

- **Web Development** was the most popular subject based on subscriber activity.
- Web Development courses also had the highest average course price among the subjects analyzed.
- Courses categorized as **All Levels** attracted the largest subscriber audience.
- **Business Finance** courses had the longest average content duration.
- Course ratings were relatively similar across the major subject areas analyzed.

The results suggested that Web Development represented a particularly strong area of consumer demand within the dataset.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Explore opportunities to optimize pricing within high-demand course categories such as **Web Development**.
- Continue offering courses accessible to **all experience levels**, which represented the largest subscriber audience.
- Evaluate course duration alongside subscriber behavior and ratings when developing future course offerings.
- Use course subject, subscriber demand, and pricing together when evaluating revenue opportunities.

---

# 🏨 Project 2: Travel + Leisure World's Best Hotels Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzed **Travel + Leisure World's Best Hotels 2022** data to explore hotel rankings across geographic regions and hotel themes.

The analysis focused on identifying patterns among highly ranked hotels and understanding which combinations of location and hotel type were associated with stronger rankings and scores.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The analysis explored the question:

> **Where are the highest-ranked hotels located, and what characteristics do they share?**

The project examined:

- Hotel rankings
- Geographic regions
- Hotel themes
- Hotel scores
- Relationships between region and hotel type

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared and analyzed using **Google Sheets** before visualization in Tableau.

Data preparation included:

- Reviewing the dataset for consistency
- Organizing hotel categories and geographic information
- Using pivot tables to explore relationships between variables
- Preparing the data for visualization and comparative analysis

---

## 📊 Analysis & Visualization

The analysis compared hotel performance across:

- Geographic regions
- Hotel themes
- Hotel rankings
- Hotel scores
- Region and theme combinations

**Google Sheets** was used for exploratory analysis, while **Tableau** was used to visualize patterns across the hotel dataset.

---

## 🔎 Key Findings

The analysis identified several patterns:

- Hotels categorized as **Southeast Asian Island** properties represented the highest-ranked region/theme combination in the dataset.
- Geographic location and hotel theme appeared to be useful dimensions for comparing hotel performance.
- The analysis showed variation in hotel rankings and scores across different region and theme combinations.

These findings helped identify characteristics shared by some of the highest-performing hotels represented in the dataset.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Use both **geographic region and hotel theme** when evaluating competitive hotel performance.
- Examine characteristics associated with highly ranked Southeast Asian Island properties when researching hospitality trends.
- Consider ranking and score together rather than relying on a single performance indicator.
- Continue analyzing regional and experiential hotel categories to identify patterns in traveler preferences.

---
