# 🎓 Udemy Course & Revenue Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Udemy course data to identify patterns in course performance and explore opportunities for increasing revenue.

Using **Google Sheets and Tableau**, I cleaned and analyzed course data across subject areas, pricing, subscriber volume, course level, content duration, and ratings. The findings were then translated into an interactive Tableau dashboard and business-focused recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary business question guiding the analysis was:

> **How and where can Udemy increase revenue?**

The analysis explored several supporting questions:

- Which course subjects attract the most subscribers?
- How does course level relate to subscriber volume?
- How do course prices vary across subjects?
- How does content duration vary by subject?
- What patterns exist between course ratings, subjects, and subscriber activity?
- Which areas may present opportunities for revenue growth?

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared for analysis using **Google Sheets**.

Data preparation included:

- Removing duplicate records
- Removing blank records
- Standardizing column headers
- Standardizing course-level categories
- Reviewing the dataset for consistency

Pivot tables and charts were then created in Google Sheets to explore relationships within the data before developing the Tableau dashboard.

---

## 🔎 Analysis

The analysis examined several dimensions of Udemy course performance, including:

- Subscriber activity by subject
- Subscriber activity by course level
- Average course price by subject and level
- Average content duration by subject
- Course ratings
- Course popularity

**Google Sheets** was used for data cleaning, exploratory analysis, pivot tables, and charts. **Tableau** was then used to visualize the findings and develop an interactive dashboard focused on subscriber demand, course duration, and pricing.

---

## 📊 Key Findings

Several patterns emerged from the analysis:

- **Web Development** was the most popular subject based on subscriber activity.
- Web Development also had the highest average course price among the subjects analyzed.
- Courses categorized as **All Levels** attracted the largest subscriber audience.
- **Business Finance** courses had the longest average content duration.
- Average course ratings were relatively similar across the major subject areas analyzed.

Overall, the analysis identified **Web Development as a particularly strong area of consumer demand** within the dataset.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the findings:

- Explore opportunities to optimize pricing within high-demand categories such as **Web Development**.
- Continue offering courses accessible to **all experience levels**, which represented the largest subscriber audience.
- Evaluate course duration alongside subscriber behavior and ratings when developing future course offerings.
- Consider course subject, subscriber demand, pricing, and engagement together when evaluating potential revenue opportunities.

## 📈 Interactive Tableau Dashboard

I developed an interactive **Udemy Course Performance Dashboard in Tableau** to visualize key patterns in course content, subscriber demand, and pricing across Udemy's four subject areas.

The dashboard includes:

- **Average Content Duration by Subject** — compares the average course length across Business Finance, Graphic Design, Musical Instruments, and Web Development.
- **Average Number of Subscribers per Subject** — compares subscriber activity across the four course categories.
- **Average Course Price by Subject and Level** — examines pricing differences across All Levels, Beginner, Expert, and Intermediate courses.

Together, these visualizations provide a consolidated view of how **course subject, content duration, subscriber demand, and pricing** vary across the Udemy course catalog.
